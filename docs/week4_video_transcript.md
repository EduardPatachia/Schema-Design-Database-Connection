# Week 4: stakeholder video

[Watch the stakeholder video](week4-stakeholder-video.mp4)

The video presents the EU Medicine Shortage Tracker to national medicine
agencies, hospital and pharmacy buyers, and health researchers. It explains the
six main entities and demonstrates the five Week 3 questions:

1. Which countries have the most ongoing shortages?
2. Which alternatives are recorded for medicines in shortage?
3. Which manufacturers are connected to shortages in several countries?
4. How long do resolved shortages last at each severity level?
5. Which facilities report shortages most often?

The original demonstration used the realistic sample data: 14 countries,
12 medicines, and 15 shortages.

## Limitations presented in the video

- The sample does not contain stock quantities or numbers of affected patients.
- It is not live data and cannot prove which company's supply failed.
- The original substitute check was not country-specific.
- Prices and trade flows are not included.
- One common severity scale is assumed for all countries.

## Future work presented in the video

- Live updates from national agencies and the EMA
- Early warnings when shortages cross borders
- Alerts for hospitals and pharmacies
- A multilingual map for EU users

Assignment 5 addresses part of the first limitation by importing real source
data. The adapted substitute query now checks the same country, but the database
still cannot prove that a substitute is physically available at a pharmacy.
